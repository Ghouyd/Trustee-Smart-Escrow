# Trustee - Smart Escrow

Trustee is a team hackathon project that proposes an AI-powered smart escrow solution for protecting peer-to-peer financial transactions.

## Overview

The project introduces a smart escrow service integrated into banking applications. It securely holds the payment until the buyer confirms receiving the product or service, helping protect both parties during the transaction.

## Problem

Peer-to-peer digital transactions often depend on trust without providing a secure mechanism that protects the buyer and seller. This increases the risk of fraud, financial loss, and disputes.

## Solution

Trustee combines a secure escrow account with AI-powered fraud detection. Before completing the payment, the system analyzes the transaction, identifies potential fraud, and produces a risk score.

## Key Features

* Secure escrow account
* AI-powered fraud detection
* Transaction risk scoring
* Buyer and seller protection
* Identity verification support
* Smart dispute management
* Integration with banking applications

## AI Model

* **Model:** LightGBM Classifier
* **Dataset:** Bank Account Fraud Dataset - NeurIPS 2022
* **Records:** 51,029 financial records
* **Features:** More than 30 transaction and account features
* **Outputs:** Fraud classification and risk score
* **Prototype Accuracy:** 94%
